Frequently asked questions

What is a good ROI?
What counts as a good ROI depends heavily on the type of investment and time frame. The S&P 500 averages about 10% annually. For marketing campaigns, a 5:1 ratio (500% ROI) is often considered good. Real estate typically returns 8-12% annually. Any positive ROI means you made money.
What is the ROI formula?
ROI = (Net Profit / Cost of Investment) × 100. Net Profit is simply the final value minus total costs. For example, if you invested $5,000 and got back $8,000, your net profit is $3,000 and your ROI is 60%.
