Frequently asked questions

What is ROI?
Return on Investment (ROI) is the percentage gain or loss on an investment relative to its cost. It is calculated as (Final Value - Initial Cost) / Initial Cost × 100. A 50% ROI means you gained half your original investment back as profit.
What is annualized return?
The annualized return (also called CAGR — Compound Annual Growth Rate) converts a total return into a yearly rate, making it easy to compare investments held for different time periods. It accounts for the compounding effect over time.
What is a good ROI?
The S&P 500 has historically returned about 10% annually before inflation. A good ROI depends on the investment type and risk level. Real estate averages 8-12%, bonds 2-5%, and savings accounts 4-5% currently.
