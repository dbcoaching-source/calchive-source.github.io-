Frequently asked questions

How do I convert hourly to annual salary?
Multiply your hourly rate by hours per week, then multiply by 52 weeks. For a standard 40-hour week: hourly rate × 40 × 52 = annual salary. A $25/hour rate equals $52,000 per year.
What is the federal minimum wage?
The federal minimum wage is $7.25 per hour as of 2025. Many states and cities have higher minimum wages. Always check your state's minimum wage as it may be higher than the federal rate.
