Frequently asked questions

What is a good car loan interest rate?
Car loan rates vary by credit score and whether the car is new or used. As of 2025, good credit borrowers can expect 5-7% on new cars and 7-11% on used cars. Rates below 5% are excellent; above 15% is very high and worth shopping around.
Should I choose a shorter or longer loan term?
A shorter loan term (36-48 months) means higher monthly payments but significantly less interest paid. A longer term (72-84 months) lowers the monthly payment but you pay much more interest and risk being underwater on the loan as the car depreciates.
