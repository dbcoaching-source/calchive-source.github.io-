Frequently asked questions

When does buying make more sense than renting?
Buying generally becomes more cost-effective the longer you stay in a home. Most calculators show a break-even point between 3-7 years depending on your local market, mortgage rate, and how much rents increase annually.
What is opportunity cost in this calculator?
If you rent instead of buying, you keep your down payment invested. The opportunity cost is the investment return you would earn on that money. This is an important but often overlooked advantage of renting.
