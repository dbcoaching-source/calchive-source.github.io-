Frequently asked questions

How is a loan payment calculated?
Monthly loan payments are calculated using the standard amortization formula, which factors in your loan amount, interest rate, and term. Each payment covers the interest accrued that month plus a portion of the principal balance.
Should I choose a shorter or longer loan term?
A shorter term means higher monthly payments but less total interest paid. A longer term means lower monthly payments but more interest over time. Use the term comparison section to see the trade-off for your specific loan.
What is a good interest rate for a personal loan?
Personal loan rates typically range from 6% to 36% depending on your credit score and lender. Borrowers with excellent credit (750+) often qualify for rates under 10%. Always compare multiple lenders before accepting an offer.
