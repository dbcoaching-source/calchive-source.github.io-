Frequently asked questions

What is included in the monthly payment?
The total monthly payment includes principal and interest on the loan, plus your monthly property tax and homeowners insurance estimates. This gives you a realistic picture of your true monthly housing cost.
How does the down payment affect my mortgage?
A larger down payment reduces your loan amount, which lowers your monthly payment and total interest paid. Putting down 20% or more also eliminates the need for private mortgage insurance (PMI).
What is an amortization schedule?
An amortization schedule shows how each payment is split between paying down principal and paying interest over the life of the loan. In the early years, most of your payment goes to interest. Over time, more goes to principal.
