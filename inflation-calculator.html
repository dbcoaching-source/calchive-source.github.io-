Frequently asked questions

What is inflation?
Inflation is the rate at which the general level of prices for goods and services rises over time, causing purchasing power to fall. The US Federal Reserve targets 2% annual inflation as a healthy level for the economy.
What data does this calculator use?
This calculator uses US Consumer Price Index (CPI) data, which measures the average change in prices paid by urban consumers for a basket of goods and services. CPI data is published by the Bureau of Labor Statistics.
