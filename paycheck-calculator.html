Frequently asked questions

What is FICA tax?
FICA stands for Federal Insurance Contributions Act. It includes Social Security tax (6.2% on wages up to $176,100 in 2026) and Medicare tax (1.45% on all wages, plus an additional 0.9% on wages over $200,000). Your employer matches these contributions.
Does my 401k contribution reduce my taxes?
Yes — traditional 401k contributions are pre-tax, meaning they reduce your taxable income for federal and state income tax purposes. A $6,000 annual 401k contribution could save you $1,320 or more in federal income taxes depending on your bracket.
