Frequently asked questions

What is break even analysis?
Break even analysis determines the sales volume at which total revenue equals total costs — you are neither making a profit nor a loss. It helps businesses set prices, plan production, and understand minimum sales targets.
What is contribution margin?
Contribution margin is the selling price minus variable costs per unit. It represents how much each unit sold contributes toward covering your fixed costs. Once fixed costs are covered, the contribution margin becomes profit.
